Option B: climate change group under timeframe

Keeps today's "Climate change" radios (Present day or 2070 to 2125). Choosing "2070 to 2125" reveals a "Climate change extents" group with a checkbox for each of flood zone 2 and flood zone 3.

Option A | Option C | Overview, styling and open questions

How it works

  • The "Climate change" radios stay as they are today. "Present day" shows flood zones 2 and 3. "2070 to 2125" keeps both present day zones on the map and adds the climate change extents.
  • Both climate change checkboxes are ticked by default, so the map looks like today's single climate change extent plus the split. Users untick one to isolate a zone.
  • Try the Present day radio: the climate change group is hidden and its layers are removed from the map and key.
  • The key groups present day and climate change items, as the key does today.
{% include "design-spec/fz-climate-change-layers-v3-10-0-0/partials/embed.html" %}

What works well

  • Smallest change from today. Existing users find the same "Climate change" radios, and the new choice only appears when they ask for climate change.
  • Present day context is always on when climate change is selected, which makes it easier to see where the extent grows.
  • Keeps the menu short on mobile when "Present day" is selected.

Risks and things to test

  • Users cannot hide the present day zones while looking at climate change. Test whether anyone wants to.
  • The revealed group moves content down the menu on mobile. Check the menu does not need scrolling to reach it.
  • Climate change has two levels of choice (radio, then checkboxes). Check users understand that both boxes ticked is the same as today's extent.
  • Wording: "Climate change extents" may be too technical.

Accessibility notes

  • Revealing the group follows the GOV.UK conditional reveal pattern. Set aria-expanded and aria-controls on the radio and keep the revealed content directly after it in the DOM.
  • Announce changes to the key and map layers with an aria-live="polite" status message. For example, "Flood zone 2 with climate change added to the map".
  • Colour is not the only cue. The two climate change colours differ in lightness, every key item has a text label, and the info panel names the layer in words (see the styling rules).
