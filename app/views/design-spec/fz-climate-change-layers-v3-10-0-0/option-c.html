Option C: present day and climate change groups

! Warning The SME does not want climate change shown without present day, so this is not an option to take forward. It is kept here so it can still be reviewed.

Option C is the only design that lets a user switch on a climate change layer with no present day layer. Options A and B always keep present day on the map with climate change. See present day data and flood zone overlap for the data change this decision leads to.

Replaces the "Climate change" choice (Present day or 2070 to 2125) with two groups of checkboxes, "Present day" and "Climate change (2070 to 2125)". Each group has flood zone 2 and flood zone 3.

Option A | Option B | Overview, styling and open questions

How it works

  • Four independent checkboxes, so any combination of present day and climate change extents can be shown.
  • No hidden or disabled states. What is ticked is what is on the map.
  • The key uses the same two groups as the menu and only lists the layers that are switched on.
{% include "design-spec/fz-climate-change-layers-v3-10-0-0/partials/embed.html" %}

What works well

  • Simplest mental model and the most flexible. Users can compare, for example, present day zone 3 with zone 2 climate change. This flexibility includes climate change on its own, which the SME does not want.
  • Simplest to build: four layers, four checkboxes, no dependencies between them.
  • Menu and key use the same grouping and wording.

Risks and things to test

  • Climate change can be shown without present day. The SME does not want this, which is why this is not an option.
  • "Flood zone 2" appears twice in the menu, so the group legends carry the meaning. Check users do not tick the wrong one.
  • Most flexible means most chance of cluttered maps with all four on. The overlap styling matters most here.
  • The longest menu of the three options, which matters on mobile.
  • Today, choosing "2070 to 2125" changes the whole map in one step. Users who want that now tick two boxes.

Accessibility notes

  • Each group is a fieldset with its own legend. The legend is read out with each checkbox, so screen reader users hear "Climate change (2070 to 2125), Flood zone 2".
  • Colour is not the only cue. The two climate change colours differ in lightness, every key item has a text label, and the info panel names the layer in words (see the styling rules).
